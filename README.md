# IoTech - QualiTrack Project Report

## Overview

IoTech is a technology startup dedicated to developing Internet of Things (IoT) solutions for organizations across various industries. Its approach focuses on integrating smart devices, sensors, connectivity, software, and data processing to support the monitoring, control, and automation of processes requiring constant information and appropriate responses.

## QualiTrack

QualiTrack is a project idea designed for quality management and control in pharmaceutical laboratories. Its objective is to digitize the supervision of manufacturing processes using IoT devices, facilitate compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), reduce human error, and improve the traceability of information generated during laboratory operations.
